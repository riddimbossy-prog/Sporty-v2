Account privacy

Clear, limited activity data.

sporty.codes uses a small amount of account activity to keep sign-ins working, understand live usage and protect the service.

Always recorded after sign-in

Sign-in time, last seen time, current sporty.codes page, device class, browser, operating system, language, time zone and sign-in method.

Optional location

Approximate location is collected only when you tick the location box and approve the browser prompt. Coordinates are rounded to a broad city area before storage.

Not collected here

Exact street addresses, precise GPS history, passwords, private sportsbook credentials and private sportsbook account information are not stored in the activity dashboard.

Online status

An account is shown as online when the site received activity within the last two minutes. Closing the site may take up to two minutes to appear offline.

Account emails

Confirmation, recovery and password-change notices are branded as sporty.codes and sent from the configured sporty.codes sender. Passwords and recovery secrets are never included in those emails.

Your choice

You can use an account without sharing location. If location permission is denied, the service continues using only non-location activity such as time zone and device class.
