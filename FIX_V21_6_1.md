# Sporty.codes v21.6.1 — Smart Board date recovery

## Fixed

- SportyBet nested booking responses now inherit the event fixture, league, market, and kickoff date into child selections.
- Today/Tomorrow labels are preserved when the date lives on the event object instead of the selection object.
- Collector status now reports tips with and without a usable kickoff.
- Smart Board no longer disappears without explanation when repeated tips are waiting for date recovery.
- No real-money staking, wallet funding, deposits, payments, or withdrawals were added.
