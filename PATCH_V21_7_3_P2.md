# Sporty.codes v21.7.3-P2 — Board/share integrity patch

Small overlay for the working v21.7.3 + P1 build.

## Fixes
- Rejects malformed merged selections that contain several fixtures/markets in one row.
- Cleans those legacy rows from the local prediction slip automatically.
- Clips long fixture and market text safely in branded share images.
- Recalculates share-card totals from valid selections only.
- Keeps the hero, status badge, section heading and stat label synchronized with Today, Tomorrow, Next 7 days, All days or Date pending.
- Bumps the PWA cache so installed apps receive the corrected JavaScript.

No collector, API, ranking threshold, wallet, payment or real-money logic is changed.
