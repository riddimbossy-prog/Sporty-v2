Privacy

Your account activity, explained simply.

sporty.codes uses basic account and session information to keep members signed in, protect accounts and show the administrator how the service is being used.

Account details

Your email address, display name, username and sign-in provider are used to operate your account. Administrators cannot see your password.

Session activity

The service records sign-in time, last active time, device class, browser, operating system, time zone and the current sporty.codes page.

No browser location prompt

sporty.codes does not request GPS or browser location permission. Precise location is not shown in the member account or administrator dashboard.

Online status

A member is shown as online for a short period after recent activity. Closing the site may take a little time to appear offline.

Saved preferences

Your selected markets, leagues, odds range, slip size, saved items and recently opened codes may be synchronized to your account so they work across signed-in devices.

In-app summaries

You control whether preference matches and saved-item changes appear inside your account. This release does not send promotional betting emails.

Retention

Live presence is intended for short-term operations, detailed activity for up to 30 days and security sign-in records for up to 90 days.

Your controls

You can review recent sign-ins, sign out every device and request account deletion from your account page.
